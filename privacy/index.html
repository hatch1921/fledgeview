Privacy

FledgeView by HatchApps LLC · Effective November 20, 2026

Short version: your photos stay on your computer. FledgeView has no account, uploads nothing, and the app itself collects nothing about you.

Your photos and edits

FledgeView, both the free version at fledgeview.com and FledgeView for Windows, reads your photos directly from your computer or memory card and works on them there. Photos are never sent to HatchApps LLC or anyone else. Ratings, edits, presets and settings are kept on your computer: in your browser's storage for the website version, and in the program's settings folder for the desktop program. Exports, copies and .xmp sidecars are written only to folders you choose.

No account, no tracking in the app

FledgeView doesn't ask for your name, email or any account. The app has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it doesn't send usage data anywhere. It reads the information stored in your photos (camera settings, dates, lens, and GPS location if your camera recorded it) only to show it to you and to sort and export your photos; that information stays on your computer. Exports remove location by default, and the export settings let you choose what to keep.

This website

fledgeview.com counts visits and downloads with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies and collects no personal data, so we can see roughly how many people find FledgeView useful. The website is hosted by Render, which, like any web host, handles the technical information browsers send when loading a page (such as IP address) to deliver the site.

FledgeView for Windows and the Microsoft Store

FledgeView for Windows is sold through the Microsoft Store. Microsoft handles the purchase, payment, refunds and licensing; HatchApps LLC never sees your payment details. When the program starts, it asks Windows whether your Microsoft account owns FledgeView; that check is between Windows and Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Microsoft gives developers summary reports (such as numbers of installs, ratings and crash reports) that don't identify you to us.

Links and tips

Links to other sites, such as Ko-fi for optional tips, YouTube or frankhatcher.com, open those sites, which have their own privacy policies. If you leave a tip, Ko-fi and its payment providers handle it.

Children

FledgeView is a general photography tool. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions: support@hatchappsllc.com.
