Culling

Bursts in minutes, not evenings

FledgeView groups each burst and scores how sharp every frame is, so the keeper stands out.

  • 4-up shows four frames side by side, zoomed together. Press P to pick the best and move on.
  • Auto-pick sharpest chooses the keeper from every burst for you.
  • Rate with the keyboard: 1 to 5, P to pick, X to reject.
Quick finish

Simple edits, done well

A slide-out panel with the adjustments most photos need: exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, whites and blacks, color, sharpening, and vignette.

  • A live histogram, with optional warnings for blown highlights and crushed blacks.
  • Copy edits and paste them onto a whole series at once.
  • Your originals never change. Edits apply when you export.
Sharing

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes, every feature, with no account and no trial. If FledgeView saves you time, you can leave a tip on Ko-fi. Tips are optional and nothing is locked.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. FledgeView runs in your browser and reads photos directly from your computer. There’s no server to send them to. Some browsers (Safari and Firefox) label their folder button “Upload,” but that’s the browser’s wording: your photos never leave your computer.

Which browsers does it work in?

Everything works in Chrome and Edge, on Windows and Mac, including moving files and saving to folders. Safari and Firefox can view, rate, edit, and export, but can’t move files.

Does it work with RAW files?

Yes, for viewing and culling: FledgeView shows the preview your camera stores inside each RAW file, and writes ratings to .xmp sidecars for Lightroom. It isn’t a raw developer, so do heavy editing in Lightroom, DxO, or similar. Quick finish works best on JPEGs.

I use Lightroom. Will moving files cause problems?

Organize in FledgeView before you import into Lightroom and everything lines up. If photos were already imported, Lightroom marks them as missing after a move; FledgeView explains how to relink them, and Undo puts everything back.

Can I use it offline, or as an app?

Yes. After your first visit, fledgeview.com works without internet, and Chrome or Edge can install it from the address bar. You can also download the single file and open it on your computer.

Where are my ratings and edits kept?

In your browser on that computer. Clearing your browsing data removes them, so use Write ratings, Move picks and rejects, or exports for a lasting record.
