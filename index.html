Import

From card to organized folders in one step

Choose your card and your main photo folder, and FledgeView copies everything into folders by date and lens, with teleconverter shots in their own subfolder.

  • Use a preset, or your own pattern to match the folder system you already have.
  • Optional backup: a second, verified copy to another drive at the same time.
  • Every copy is checked, photos already imported are skipped, and the card is never changed.
  • Then cull the new folders and bring them into Lightroom whenever you like.
Culling

Bursts in minutes, not evenings

FledgeView groups each burst and scores how sharp every frame is, so the keeper stands out.

  • 4-up and Compare show frames side by side, zoomed together. Click any frame to pick, reject, or rate it.
  • Sharpness measured where your camera focused (on Sony files straight from the camera), so the sharp eye wins, not the busy background. Auto-pick sharpest chooses the keeper from every burst.
  • Filter by lens, camera, or teleconverter, and rate with the keyboard: 1 to 5, P to pick, X to reject.
Quick finish

Simple edits, done well

A slide-out panel with the adjustments most photos need: exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, whites and blacks, color, sharpening, and vignette.

  • A live histogram, with optional warnings for blown highlights and crushed blacks.
  • Black & white with classic film filters, from neutral to dramatic red-filter skies.
  • Select a series and paste the same edits onto all of it at once.
  • Your originals never change. Edits apply when you export.
Sharing

Export the right size for Instagram, Stories and Reels, Facebook, and YouTube in one step, each with its own crop, starting on your subject.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes, every feature, with no account and no trial. If FledgeView saves you time, you can leave a tip on Ko-fi. Tips are optional and nothing is locked.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. FledgeView runs in your browser and reads photos directly from your computer. There’s no server to send them to. Some browsers word their folder dialog as “Upload” or ask whether to “upload files to this site” (Safari, Firefox, and some Chrome-based browsers such as Brave). That’s only the browser’s standard wording for letting a page read files: FledgeView reads them right there in your browser, and your photos never leave your computer.

Does FledgeView track me?

The app never sends anything anywhere: no analytics, no tracking, and your photos and ratings stay on your computer. This website counts visits and downloads anonymously with GoatCounter, without cookies or any personal data, so I can see roughly how many people find FledgeView useful.

Can it copy straight from my memory card?

Yes. Import copies a card (or any folder) into your main photo folder, sorted by date and lens, or by your own folder pattern. Every copy is checked, photos you’ve already imported are skipped, and nothing on the card is changed or deleted. Import works in Chrome and Edge.

Which browsers does it work in?

Everything works in Chrome and Edge, on Windows and Mac, including Import, moving files, and saving to folders. Safari and Firefox can view, rate, edit, and export, but can’t import or move files.

Does it work with RAW files?

Yes, for viewing and culling: FledgeView shows the preview your camera stores inside each RAW file, and writes ratings to .xmp sidecars for Lightroom. It isn’t a raw developer, so do heavy editing in Lightroom, DxO, or similar. Quick finish works best on JPEGs.

I use Lightroom. Will moving files cause problems?

Import and organize in FledgeView first, then bring the folders into Lightroom, and everything lines up. If photos were already imported, Lightroom marks them as missing after a move; FledgeView explains how to relink them, and Undo puts everything back.

Can I use it offline, or as an app?

Yes. After your first visit, fledgeview.com works without internet, and Chrome or Edge can install it from the address bar. You can also download the single file and open it on your computer.

Does FledgeView delete my rejects?

No. Rejects move into a _rejects folder next to your photos, and you can move just the rejects, just the picks, or both. Once you’re sure, delete that folder yourself in File Explorer or Finder, which sends it to the Recycle Bin or Trash.

Where are my ratings and edits kept?

In your browser on that computer. Clearing your browsing data removes them, so use Write ratings, Move picks and rejects, or exports for a lasting record.
